description: Rewrite and select CV or resume content from a sourced private profile without changing the underlying claims. Use when wording, emphasis, length or audience changes.
---

## Outcome

Give the reader a coherent account of the person's work. A full CV preserves
the reviewed detail; a resume makes a deliberate selection for its audience.
Neither is a visual style. Done means every retained claim has evidence and
substantial omissions or changes of emphasis are recorded separately.

## Inputs

Read the owner's brief, canonical profile, source CV and existing writing
guidance. Preserve originals. Resolve conflicting facts before selecting them.
Ask only for facts needed for the next deliverable; do not re-ask answered
questions or ask an owner to retype reliable public metadata.

## Workflow

Rewrite awkward expression when requested, including a brief whose general
formatting template says otherwise but whose specific content instructions
explicitly require a rewrite. Connect sentences with supported relationships.
Use concrete verbs and explain technical work in ordinary professional language.
Keep official titles and publication titles accurate. Do not manufacture metrics,
results or causal links to improve a sentence or balance bullet lengths.

Keep stable factual entries and evidence in one private career profile. Keep
reviewed variant wording, inclusion and order in separate authoritative QMD
files. These authorities have different jobs: the profile supports the claims;
QMD controls what each document says. Use stable entry IDs to link them.

JSON selections and display overrides may seed the first QMD draft, but they
are not ongoing render inputs. After review, edit QMD directly. A new fact
updates evidence/profile and the affected QMD after review; a wording-only edit
need not rewrite the fuller career record. Refreshes propose diffs and must not
overwrite edited QMD. Styles and generated DOCX/PDF do not own narrative content.

For a short resume, select before shrinking. Omit a generic profile if it adds
little. Preserve the relative order of retained sections from the owner's brief.
A one-page version is a requested output, not a universal rule for senior people.
Keep the full chronology and details in the longer CV. List meaningful omissions
in a review note instead of silently deleting them from the canonical record.

Place sustained leadership prominently once when it spans several roles.
Use supported activities and project examples rather than repeating the role
list already shown in the chronology. Describe distinct regional results together rather than repeating them under
every job. Keep dates, headcounts and budget ranges attached to their actual
scope. Do not assign undated responsibilities to a particular appointment.

Preserve shared credit and software maturity. Keep ongoing work distinct from
completed results. Separate published and accepted journal articles, reports,
software and policy writing. Group working-paper and journal versions of the
same study; a software output may remain distinct. Do not invent final journal
metadata before publication. Sources suggest facts; they do not approve them.

## Verification

Review the prose as a reader, then reconcile it with the source evidence. Record
selection decisions, unresolved metadata and factual corrections outside the
outward document. Use [../cv-qa/SKILL.md](../cv-qa/SKILL.md) for delivery checks.
