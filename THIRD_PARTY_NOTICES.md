# Distribution and licensing notes

Toolkit code, synthetic starter QMD, generated Word style reference and generic
presentation helpers are covered by the repository MIT license. The shared
rendering engine was ported from the same author's reviewed project renderer;
its original Windows job/locking and output-retention logic is retained.

No font binaries, Quarto, Pandoc or TeX distribution are bundled. They keep their
own licenses. The starter PDF declares the TeX Helvetica-compatible sans-serif
family supplied by a configured TeX distribution; the Word reference declares
Arial by name, without embedding a font. Word's installed-font behavior and
pagination are application-specific. Existing private projects keep their own
presentation and licensing requirements. No Quarto Vitae template is bundled.

All shipped starter identities, employers, evidence and claims are fictional
Alex Example placeholders. They must be replaced before sharing a real CV.
No private career data or documents are part of the distribution.
